# ⚔️ Roblox Combat System

## Overview

The **Combat System** is a melee combat system developed in Roblox Studio, designed to provide players with an interactive and responsive fighting experience. The system incorporates a combination of light attacks, heavy attacks, defensive mechanics, and timed blocking to create a varied combat experience.

Players can perform a sequence of seven M1 light attacks, each with its own animation, alongside an M2 heavy attack. Defensive mechanics include blocking, perfect blocking, and guard breaking, allowing players to balance offensive pressure with well-timed defensive actions.

The system also features hit effects that trigger when attacks successfully connect with a target, providing visual feedback and improving the overall feel of combat.

## Features

- **Seven M1 Light Attacks** – Includes a seven-hit light attack sequence with animations for each attack, creating a combo-based combat system.
- **M2 Heavy Attack** – Provides an additional attack option to complement the M1 combo system.
- **Hit Effects** – Triggers visual effects when an attack successfully hits a target, providing immediate feedback during combat.
- **Blocking System** – Allows players to defend against incoming attacks and reduce or prevent damage while guarding.
- **Perfect Block** – Rewards players for blocking at the correct moment, encouraging precise timing and defensive skill.
- **Guard Break** – Allows a guard to be broken under specified combat conditions, creating an opportunity to punish defensive opponents.
- **Combat Animations** – Uses animations to communicate attack sequences and defensive actions, making combat more visually engaging.
- **Combo-Based Combat** – Combines light attacks, heavy attacks, and defensive mechanics to create opportunities for different combat strategies.

## Development

### Engine
- **Game Engine:** Roblox Studio
- **Platform:** Roblox

### Language
- **Primary Language:** Luau (Roblox's scripting language)

### Development Components

- **Combat Logic:** Manages attack inputs, attack sequences, and interactions between offensive and defensive mechanics.
- **Animation System:** Plays the appropriate animations for the seven M1 attacks, M2 heavy attack, blocking, perfect blocking, and guard breaking.
- **Hit Detection:** Determines when attacks connect with targets and triggers the corresponding combat effects.
- **Visual Effects:** Handles hit effects to provide visual confirmation when attacks successfully land.
- **Defensive Mechanics:** Processes blocking, perfect block timing, and guard break interactions.
- **Combo Management:** Tracks the M1 attack sequence and coordinates transitions between individual attacks.
- **Combat State Management:** Coordinates attack and defensive states to maintain consistent interactions between combat actions.



*This project demonstrates the development of a melee combat system in Roblox Studio, combining combo-based attacks, animation-driven combat, visual hit feedback, and timing-based defensive mechanics.*
